About Lumen

Lumen is a place to discover movies: browse what's popular, search by title, genre or year, watch trailers, and keep a list of what you want to see next.

What you can do here

  • Browse rows of popular, top-rated and new movies, with a trailer playing on the home page.
  • Search by title and filter by genre or year.
  • Open any movie for its poster, story, rating, genres and trailer.
  • Create a free account to build your own My List and pick up where you left off with Continue watching.

About the films

Lumen is a catalogue, not a pirate site. Movie information comes from TMDB, trailers play from their official YouTube uploads, and a full film is only shown when there is a licensed source for it, such as a public-domain or Creative Commons release. Where a film is not available here, you will not find a copy of it on this site.

How it's built

The pages use plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Behind them is a Node.js and Express server with a MySQL database. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, never as plain text. The site runs on Cloudflare, Render and Aiven.
